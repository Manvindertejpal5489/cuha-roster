Notes

  1. Bars show each resident's primary allocation per day — solid = base posting, striped = duty (UG teaching or outreach van); a dark-ringed striped bar is a duty day transferred in from another resident (e.g. Dr Kiranmayi conducting Dr Pooja's Batch E sessions — see note 4). Every resident has exactly one allocation on every day. The tinted columns mark Sundays — a reading aid for the Sun–Sat outreach weeks, not a day off: IPC, PHC Saibaba and PHC Barkheda run on Sundays and each van week begins on one. IPC is manned by ONE resident a day from 04 Sep — Dr Manvinder (SR) whenever he is present, and otherwise the Junior Resident designated for the month per the departmental roster (Dr Abhishek: September–October; Dr Prerak: November–December), who is Field-based on the days Dr Manvinder is at IPC (the blue IPC blocks on his row mark exactly the days he mans it). When both are away on approved teaching, outreach-van or leave duty the day falls to Field Activities, and a named resident from that group mans IPC for the whole day — Dr Chhavi on 19–21 Nov, the only such days in the window. 01–03 Sep keep the earlier two-resident posting as circulated: Dr Chhavi (01 & 03 Sep) and Dr Kiranmayi (02 Sep) held the Senior Resident slot alongside Dr Abhishek while Dr Manvinder was on the van. PHC Saibaba rotates WEEKLY (Sun–Sat) between Dr Kiranmayi & Dr Pooja — a week blocked by van/teaching/leave passes to the other (both blocked → a Field Activities JR looped in temporarily; never needed in this window). PHC Barkheda rotates monthly between Dr Chaitra & Dr Akhil; within September the post is held by Dr Yash on 01 Sep and Dr Prerak 02–05 Sep (covering for Dr Akhil while he teaches), Dr Akhil 06–14 Sep and Dr Chaitra 15–30 Sep, with Dr Athira (JR) posted there as named support 06–30 Sep — she holds the centre alone while Dr Akhil is on the outreach van 06–12 Sep and Dr Chaitra 27 Sep–03 Oct (see note 4).
  2. MBBS teaching is ONE Senior Resident per batch (orders dt 29/07/26 & 01/07/26); PGs from Field Activities aid as per requirement — no PG is ever the sole teacher.
  3. Yellow Fever Wednesdays at IPC — attended by the day's IPC resident. Dr Manvinder on his nine free Wednesdays (09 Sep; 14 & 28 Oct; 04, 11 & 18 Nov; 16, 23 & 30 Dec); Dr Abhishek on 16, 23 & 30 Sep and 07 & 21 Oct; Dr Prerak on 25 Nov and 02 & 09 Dec. On 02 Sep, before the single-resident rule, Dr Kiranmayi held the IPC Senior Resident slot alongside Dr Abhishek. Outreach van days are not interchanged for this: Dr Manvinder keeps both van weeks whole (02 Sep, 07 Oct), and Dr Prerak keeps his whole 15–21 Nov van week — Dr Manvinder mans IPC on 18 Nov.
  4. Approved leave in this window. Dr Pooja 01–14 Sep; she resumes at PHC Saibaba from 15 Sep. Dr Kiranmayi covers her for the entire leave — administrative holder of PHC Saibaba 01–14 Sep and conducting the Batch E sessions on 01, 03–05, 07–12 & 14 Sep. Those sessions are her primary allocation, so her Saibaba bar shows on the Sundays 06 & 13 Sep, when no session runs; on Wednesday 02 Sep she is at IPC instead (see note 3) and that day's Batch E session is unassigned — no other Senior Resident is free and a PG is never the sole teacher (note 2); the centre is held on session days by the named JR support — Dr Athira, posted to PHC Saibaba 01–02 Sep, Dr Angel in her place 03–05 Sep while Dr Athira is herself on leave, and Dr Prerak 06–14 Sep, who moves there from PHC Barkheda on 06 Sep. Dr Chaitra is on leave 01–05 Sep — Dr Akhil conducts her five 9th Sem Batch C sessions on those days, so the batch is taught throughout by a Senior Resident (see note 2). Because a session occupies the whole working day, PHC Barkheda is covered for him throughout: Dr Yash holds it on 01 Sep and Dr Prerak takes over 02–05 Sep, handing back when Dr Akhil resumes PHC Barkheda 06–14 Sep — though he is on the outreach van 06–12 Sep as per the signed Outreach roster, so Dr Athira (JR), posted to PHC Barkheda 06–30 Sep until her NHM deputation begins, holds the centre that week — the same day Dr Prerak moves to PHC Saibaba. Dr Athira's own van week 20–26 Sep is carved out of that posting (Dr Chaitra holds the centre alone that week), and she holds it alone again 27–30 Sep while Dr Chaitra is on the van. Dr Chaitra resumes Barkheda from 15 Sep. Dr Prakhar is on leave 01–05 Sep, so Dr Chhavi holds the IPC Senior Resident slot on 01 & 03 Sep — every Senior Resident is committed elsewhere on those days; from 04 Sep Dr Abhishek mans IPC alone under the single-resident rule (note 1). Dr Manvinder keeps his outreach van week 30 Aug – 05 Sep intact. Dr Manvinder 21–27 Oct — Dr Abhishek mans IPC.

No duty extends past 31 Dec.
